ETKDGv3 algorithm

The ETKDGv3 algorithm (Experimental-Torsion Knowledge Distance Geometry version 3) is a standard open-source stochastic method used for 3D molecular conformer generation. Implemented natively in the RDKit cheminformatics toolkit, it produces highly realistic 3D coordinates from a flat 2D molecular structure (such as a SMILES string) by combining mathematical distance geometry with empirical rules.
